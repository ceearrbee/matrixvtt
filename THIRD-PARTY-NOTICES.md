Classes, XP tables, and spell lists derived from the Old-School Essentials

### pbta.json

The Powered by the Apocalypse framework originates in Apocalypse World by
D. Vincent Baker and Meguey Baker (http://apocalypse-world.com/). This file
credits that design lineage and reproduces none of its text; the generic basic
moves are original to MatrixVTT.

### risus.json

Fan implementation of Risus: The Anything RPG by S. John Ross, Big Dice Games
(https://www.risusrpg.com/). Mechanics-level description only.

### savage-worlds.json

Generic mechanics implementation only. Savage Worlds is a trademark of
