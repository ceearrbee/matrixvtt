Layout 7 - Canvas + on-demand drawers.

The map is a clean full canvas. Nothing permanently flanks it: the left rail is just tabs that slide a drawer in when you want it (Party shown open here), and chat lives as a slim always-glanceable ticker pinned to the bottom that expands to a full panel on demand. Most focus on the map, least chrome. Trade-off: chat & party aren't both fully visible at once - you summon what you need (closest in spirit to the "summon panels" idea, but with sensible defaults).
