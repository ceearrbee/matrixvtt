Layout 3 - Full-bleed map + floating panels.

The map is the entire stage; initiative, party, the current-turn actions, and chat float over it as movable, dockable panels (hairline + restrained blur + a real shadow, since they genuinely float). Maximizes map real-estate and is flexible. Trade-off: panels can occlude the board and it edges toward the Foundry "manage your windows" feel the brand wants to avoid - needs disciplined defaults.
